Questions about pricing?

Some useful details to help you understand our packages before choosing a plan.

Are these final prices?

These are illustrative starting prices. Actual pricing should be confirmed after reviewing your requirements, features, content, integrations and delivery scope.

Do I need to pay for hosting and a domain separately?

Hosting, domain registration, premium tools and third-party services may be billed separately unless they are explicitly included in the final written project proposal.

Can I request features that are not listed?

Yes. Share your requirements with the team. Additional features can be assessed and included in a revised scope and quote.

How long will my project take?

Delivery time depends on project size, content readiness, integrations, testing and review cycles. A timeline should be agreed after discovery.

Is ongoing maintenance included?

Ongoing maintenance is not automatically included unless the final agreement specifies it. Support options can be discussed before launch.
